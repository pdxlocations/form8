Prepare a map before connecting to your radio

Use this page on your phone or laptop while it has internet access. Download your area, save the map file, then import it into Form8 after joining the radio’s Wi-Fi.

  1. Choose your area. Use your location or enter coordinates. Zoom and move the map until the area you need fits on screen.
  2. Download and export. Choose the detail, click Download visible area, wait for Ready offline, then click Export map. Keep the JSON file in Files or Downloads.
  3. Import on the radio. Connect to its Wi-Fi, open http://192.168.4.1, then choose Map → Offline maps → Import saved map and select that file.

Saving tiles on this website does not make them available on the radio’s webpage automatically. Export the map file and import it there. If you prepare it on a laptop for a phone, transfer the file to the phone first.

    2. Download and save a map file

    The visible area is saved at the selected detail and overview zoom levels. More detail needs more tiles. Limits: 400 saved tiles / 32 MB per browser.

    Keep this page open until the download finishes, then export your map.

    Saved maps and display options

    Downloads stay in this browser. Canceling or a failed download keeps completed tiles; downloading the same area again skips saved tiles. Clearing browser data can remove them, so keep the exported file.

    3. Import after connecting to the radio

    On the device you will use in the field, join the radio’s Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1. In Map → Offline maps → Import saved map, choose form8-offline-map.json. Check the saved area and zoom levels before leaving.
